strongs-hebrew.json is derived from "A Concise Dictionary of the Words in the Hebrew Bible
with their Renderings in the King James Version" by James Strong, LL.D., S.T.D. (1894),
in the JSON edition published by Open Scriptures:

  https://github.com/openscriptures/strongs  (hebrew/strongs-hebrew-dictionary.js)

JSON version Copyright 2010, Open Scriptures. CC-BY-SA.
Editing, corrections and Unicode transliterations by David Troidl.
The XML e-text came from a source by David Instone-Brewer (www.2LetterLookup.com).

This copy keeps only the lemma, transliteration, pronunciation, derivation, Strong's
definition, and KJV renderings for each entry. Regenerate it with the one-off script in
README.md ("Static data").
