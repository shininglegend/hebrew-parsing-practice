tbesh-glosses.json is the Gloss column of the Translators Brief lexicon of Extended Strongs
for Hebrew (TBESH), created by www.STEPBible.org based on work at Tyndale House Cambridge,
licensed CC BY 4.0:

  https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data  (Lexicons/TBESH ...txt)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes from the source: Strong's numbers are written without zero padding to match the
Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible (H0430 -> H430). Where TBESH gives several rows for one number,
the common word (dStrong suffix G, or the unsuffixed row) is kept and names and compounds
sharing the number are dropped. Where TBESH splits a number into BDB homonyms (H7652a,
H7652b) and the text does not, the first split also answers for the plain number. The
Meaning column (abridged BDB, © Online Bible) is not included. Please take the data from
github.com/STEPBible rather than from this copy; scripts/build-glosses.mjs regenerates it.
